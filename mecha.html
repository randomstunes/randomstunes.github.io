Mechanimals (2024-2026)

First ever real test of my skills. Originally meant to be part of an actual commercial game, was not used due to internal disagreement, be warned, most of these songs date all the way back to 2024/2025, with some aspects in them being even older. The main inspiration for this was mid to late 90's arcade game soundtracks, it was supposed to be my debut in the industry.

The songs included here are from a planned CD style release.
Some of them have been edited/omitted to avoid any trouble with the development studio.
